TrustCard Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 16, 2026 • Data Controller: TrustCard

1. Data We Collect (Zero-PAN Commitment)

TrustCard collects only the account email address, display name, catalog card selections, and opt-in push notification or geolocation verification preferences needed to operate our Personal Finance SaaS platform. TrustCard never collects, stores, or logs full credit card numbers (PANs), CVVs, or PIN codes.

2. How Data Is Used

Account and subscription billing data are used strictly to calculate card rewards yields, verify merchant MCC safety, deliver software access immediately upon signup, and process subscription payments (appearing on credit card statements as TRUSTCARD).

3. Cancellation, Refunds & Account Deletion

You may cancel your subscription at any time in app settings with no cancellation fees, or request account deletion or a 14-day refund through TrustCard customer support.
