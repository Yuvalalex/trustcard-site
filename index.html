How TrustCard Works

1. Catalog-Based Card Matching

Users select their US rewards cards from a public catalog of reward multipliers, category caps, and foreign transaction fee profiles—never entering full card numbers.

2. Fail-Closed $0.00 Checkout Guard

When an advisory tap triggers an authorization check, TrustCard responds in under 400ms to keep balances at $0.00 while classifying merchant MCC and fraud perimeter risk.

3. Instant Optimal Card Alert

Users receive an immediate real-time notification showing the exact points/cashback multiplier and net dollar reward so they complete checkout with their optimal card.

Security, Privacy & Zero-PAN Architecture

Strict PCI-Free Isolation

TrustCard is engineered from the ground up as a non-custodial payment advisory companion:

  • No storage, logging, or transmission of 13–19 digit Primary Account Numbers (PAN), Track 2 data, CVVs, or PINs.
  • HMAC-SHA256 verified webhook envelopes and Row-Level Security (RLS) database tenant isolation.
  • Tokenized digital wallet provisioning via regulated issuing infrastructure with strict fail-closed controls.
  • Platform-compliant NFC/HCE diagnostics restricted solely to TrustCard's own authorized application identifiers.

Compliance & Merchant Disclosures

What is TrustCard's business model and service scope?

TrustCard provides software-based credit card rewards optimization, merchant category verification, and digital wallet advisory tooling for US consumers. TrustCard does not extend credit, hold consumer deposits, or process commercial merchant acquiring directly.

How are issued companion cards protected?

Any companion virtual card provisioned through regulated issuing partners operates exclusively under zero-balance advisory authorization rules. Incoming authorization webhooks evaluate merchant safety and always respond fail-closed with zero settled funds.

Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

TrustCard collects only user-provided email authentication metadata, tokenized card selections, and anonymized merchant MCC advisory metrics needed to calculate rewards yields. Users may request complete account deletion or revoke push/location permissions at any time in Settings.
