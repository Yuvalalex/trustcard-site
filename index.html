How TrustCard Works

TrustCard is delivered exclusively as a digital Personal Finance Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) web and mobile companion. No physical goods are shipped.

1. Catalog Card Portfolio

Subscribers select their credit cards from our public US rewards catalog covering category multipliers, spending caps, and foreign transaction fees—with zero raw card numbers entered.

2. Real-Time Checkout Advisor

Before or during a purchase, TrustCard evaluates merchant MCC risk, location perimeter, and yield rankings in milliseconds so members always know which card maximizes points or cashback.

3. Instant Savings Alerts

Members receive instant push and in-app telemetry notifications showing the net dollar reward value, applicable bonus caps, and fraud warnings before tapping their payment card.
